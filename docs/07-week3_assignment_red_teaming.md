# Assignment: Privacy red team your use case

!!! note "Week 3"

    Team project

It's time to think about what you'd want to break and how you might go about breaking it!

Test out either Plot4AI - https://plot4.ai/ or make your own evil plan to think through the risks you already identified.

- What are you after? What is your goal as an attacker?
- What information or knowledge might you be able to get or already have?
- How can you use that information to expose something private in the system?

Work through at least 1 or 2 scenarios as a team and mark the troublesome ones for your backlog or in your Privacy Risk Analysis.
